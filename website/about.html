About JobConnect

JobConnect is a dedicated career linkage portal built to simplify how university students and fresh graduates connect with reputable employers. Inspired by leading regional job boards, our platform prioritizes direct transparency, verified student credentials, and fast employer outreach.

Why Users Choose JobConnect

  1. Direct Employer Access: Recruiters can view uploaded resumes and contact candidates immediately without middleman delays.
  2. Verified Skill Ratings: Students showcase academic and project ratings alongside their CVs for stronger credibility.
  3. Student-Centered Design: Tailored specifically for attachments, internships, and entry-level roles.
